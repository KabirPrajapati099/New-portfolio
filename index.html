About / 03

Craft runs in the family

I'm a UI/UX designer and SEO content writer currently interning at Orbosis Global Pvt. Ltd. in Indore, where I split my time between building product design systems and writing content that's meant to actually rank. Alongside that, I'm completing a Bachelor's in Information Technology at Padmashree College — a mix that keeps me equally comfortable in Figma and in a spreadsheet of keyword data. My final year project applies machine learning to predict how healthy a packaged food product is, just from its ingredient list, and that same instinct — define the problem precisely before touching the tool — is how I approach every design brief and every article outline. Whether I'm structuring a screen flow or a heading hierarchy, the goal is the same: make something a person can move through without friction.

How I Work / 04

From brief to build

01

Research the brief

Define the real problem — user, constraint, and what "done" looks like — before opening any tool.

02

Structure the system

Tokens, type scale, spacing and states first, so every screen after the first one is faster and more consistent.

03

Prototype in Stitch AI

Draft screen-level prompts against the system, then hand-tune what the model gets close but not exact.

04

Ship & document

Hand off a living style guide — not a one-off screen — so the next person can extend it without me.
